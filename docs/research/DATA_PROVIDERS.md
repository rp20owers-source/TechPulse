# Free-data feasibility, permissions and operating limits

Reviewed **2026-11-24** against the primary sources linked below. Published terms can change. These engineering decisions are conservative product gates, not legal opinions or a declaration that every contemplated investment-research use is lawful. A free application does not automatically receive commercial-use, storage or redistribution permission. User credentials are optional; the app's offline/import workflow remains usable without them.

## Adapter guarantees and deliberate limits

All three adapters use HTTPX with a shared monotonic 60-second cooperative operation budget across pages, retries and rate waits. Each network phase is limited to the lesser of five seconds or its remaining budget; budget checks run between decoded chunks and before returning results. This is not hard thread cancellation: an in-flight blocking phase may finish up to its at-most-five-second timeout after the budget is exhausted. They permit at most three attempts, follow no redirects, limit decompressed responses to 8 MiB and enforce process-local per-host rate gates. Short `Retry-After` delays are honored only within budget; cooldowns over ten seconds surface a retry-later error. No headers, credential values, response bodies or raw request exceptions appear in diagnostics. Credentials are never logged or persisted by this module. Callers should close adapters or use a context manager.

`AlpacaProvider.daily_bars` explicitly requests `feed=iex`, `adjustment=all`, 1Day bars and inclusive date-only range. It follows page tokens across multiple symbols, rejects nonadvancing tokens, duplicate dates, invalid/nonfinite OHLCV and unexpected symbols, and raises instead of returning partial history when the page budget is reached. Historical revisions, dividends and adjusted prices are unsuitable for pretending to have a point-in-time strategy database. Liquidity is IEX-feed liquidity, not consolidated tape liquidity.

`CoinGeckoProvider.markets` intentionally fetches only one explicitly bounded top-100 snapshot; it does not claim complete-market gainers or Bitcoin dominance. Coin ID is retained because ticker symbols can collide. Stablecoin/memecoin classifications are not inferred from price or names. Missing prices, history and volume remain unknown. The current application must decide whether lawful personal session use is enabled; the presence of a code adapter is not provider approval of an app's use.

`USAspendingProvider.search` intentionally returns at most 100 recent-starting US prime contract awards matching a keyword. It does not paginate a global dataset, map tickers, promise recent announcement coverage, or substitute contract ceilings for obligations. Parent/child/shared awards and transaction modifications must not be summed without award-level reconciliation. Original search URL and globally generated award ID are retained where provided. Unknown announcement date, ceiling, technology and future expansion remain unknown.

## Acceptance gates before wider coverage

1. Save and review applicable user/provider terms and exact account entitlement; confirm storage, derived-data and export scope. Do not add paid fallbacks.
2. Add provider-specific cache TTL, deletion and encryption requirements before retaining licensed observations; local-first does not mean indefinite retention rights.
3. Implement per-provider monthly-budget accounting before unattended refreshes. Current refreshes are user-driven, bounded and conservative, not a quota-management guarantee across machines/processes.
4. Verify legal basis before political automation, public record distribution or derivative political portfolios. An open-source license covers app code, not disclosure records.
5. Validate current live endpoint schema with user-authorized credentials before calling an adapter production verified. Unit tests use synthetic HTTP fixtures and do not establish live service availability or data accuracy.
