# Independent product and requirements review

Reviewer: Codex research/product subagent. Review date: 2026-11-24. Scope: read-only inspection of the lead's integrated working tree under `outputs/TechPulse`, including `app.py`, `research.py`, `reports.py`, `store.py`, `demo.py`, entry point, architecture and README. This is a preview review, not a completed acceptance audit of all 65 requirements. The lead was concurrently making integration fixes; findings must be reconciled against the final checkpoint.

## Assessment

The implementation is a native local research **preview**, with substantive offline/import workflows and unusually explicit data/evidence limits. The README correctly disclaims completion of the full 65-requirement production product. All 16 requested navigation destinations have handlers. The dashboard distinguishes fictional fixtures from personal research; automatic political collection, complete options flow and calibrated forecasts are explicitly unavailable. These boundaries are appropriate.

The main remaining product gap is the brief's integrated opportunity-discovery mission. The current `opportunity()` path only supplies technical and reliability components; fundamentals, catalysts, options and political components remain absent. With the default weights only 35% of weighted evidence can be present, and independent-component/fundamentals gates prevent candidate eligibility even if weights are changed. The app can rank observations and abstain, but it cannot yet generate an eligible multi-source candidate from imported evidence. That is a documented preview limitation, not a tested completed opportunity engine.

## Requirement coverage implications

These groups complement the lead's item-by-item coverage matrix; they do not replace it.

- **Equities (1–10):** local imported-history movers, returns, relative volume, categories, market-cap/liquidity filters and watchlists are substantive. Complete automatic market discovery, a verified sector benchmark, point-in-time fundamentals, broad earnings/event monitoring and justified undervaluation selection remain absent or partial. Five and 21 observations must continue to be labelled as observations rather than guaranteed calendar periods.
- **Crypto (11–18):** independent workspace and asset classifications/correlation can operate on imported history. Current direct-provider results are session-only previews. Global dominance cannot be inferred from a local/truncated universe and is correctly unavailable; deep liquidity, category index construction and integrated automatic crypto discovery remain partial.
- **Options (19–26):** authorized imports and deterministic trade/aggregate metrics exist, with explicit unknown intent. Comprehensive live trade discovery, historical feed completeness and corporate-event linkage are not implemented. Preserve actual size/price/multiplier semantics; test arithmetic separately from predictive usefulness.
- **Political disclosures (27–33):** separate Pelosi/Trump workspaces, normalized owner/date/range fields and post-publication performance are useful scaffolding. Automated official retrieval is disabled pending lawful-use clearance. No actual political records are bundled. An imported observation is not endorsement or real-time access; disclosure-inspired strategy validation remains deferred.
- **Contracts (34–40):** imported award observations and optional official search support research. Ceilings/obligations and deduplication boundaries are clear. Search results have no automatic verified ticker/subsidiary mapping; international monitoring, confirmed subcontractors, duration/expansion economics and investment implications require further work. Raw record display and obligation ratios do not alone meet the entire mandated analyst-report specification.
- **Technical analysis (41–50):** deterministic indicators and some objective exploratory pattern families exist. Flags, pennants, cup-and-handle and candlesticks are deferred per technical research. The fixed SMA chronological diagnostic is not a pattern-specific historical validation suite. Abstaining on forecasts with unavailable confidence is correct.
- **Opportunities (51–58):** configurable weighting, missingness, explicit abstention and a manual paper ledger are implemented foundations. Multi-source candidate/thesis generation, a historical recommendation archive and cash-flow-matched benchmark evaluation remain deferred. Manual simulated fills are not recommendation performance.
- **Reports/production (59–65):** local report snapshots, archive, four serialization formats, in-app Monday catch-up and backups are meaningful. Catch-up uses stored data and does not fabricate old reports or refresh feeds. Polished briefs, sustained data operations, complete license/security review, clean-machine installers and verified public release acceptance are separate gates.

## Verification scope

Static inspection, luminance calculation and isolated runtime checks were executed. No live market requests were made during this review. Temporary state and captures stayed in the reviewer's worktree, outside deliverables and Git.

- Native Windows Qt backend: all 16 pages in each of the two workspaces opened (32 combinations), with no recorded page errors. Movers search reduced eight fictional equities to the exact SYN-CHIP match. Empty personal research contained no fictional assets.
- Visually inspected dark dashboard and light movers at 1480×940 logical size, and options/empty research at 1100×720. Typography, spacing, banners and scrollable tables were readable. Large datasets, high DPI across monitors, screen readers and keyboard-only workflows were not comprehensively evaluated. Tables and the 16-item sidebar need scrolling at smaller sizes; no claim of mobile responsiveness is appropriate.
- Root's fixes for provider state were retested using fake functions (no network): failed request clears old preview and exposes the error; delayed response is discarded after changing to fictional workspace.
- Root's provenance fix was retested by importing asset metadata from fictional source A and a price bar from fictional source B. Row and report sources retained B as price provenance separately from A.
- Root's light-theme positive/negative changes were inspected and recalculated: `#116f61` and `#b72848` produce approximately 6.05:1 and 6.16:1 against white. The original light-text contrast finding is resolved for those fields; this is not a full accessibility certification.

Review harness failures caused by a concurrently renamed scroll attribute, deferred Qt widget deletion and the newly required currency field were corrected in the temporary harness, then checks rerun successfully. They were not reported as app defects.

At the initial review checkpoint, provider-state, price-provenance and identified light-text contrast findings were **resolved and retested**. The initial table ranking defect was subsequently **resolved and independently retested at main ccdc750**: supplied B/A/C row order was preserved, actual header clicks sorted numeric values 2/10/30 and toggled direction, and movers showed SYN-AI first by five-period performance. Fresh native dashboard/movers captures were inspected. Report renderer improvements are being handled separately by the lead and were not certified here. Core multi-source discovery, empirical validation and production release gates remain scope limitations. See RELEASE_READINESS.md for the binary-publication gates.

## Release-facing wording

Use **research preview** consistently. Appropriate claims include "authorized local imports," "deterministic indicators," "exploratory pattern observations," "session-only optional provider previews," and "manual paper ledger." Do not call the preview a complete free-data terminal, validated forecasting system, automatic political tracker, comprehensive options scanner or proven recommendation engine. Source code publication, build success, native smoke tests and clean-machine installer validation must each be reported from their own observed evidence.
